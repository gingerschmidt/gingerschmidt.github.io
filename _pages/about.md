I'm a PhD candidate in Medical Engineering & Medical Physics through the [Harvard-MIT HST](https://hst.mit.edu/) program, graduating in May 2027. I am currently seeking postdoctoral research opportunities.

I'm excited about bringing mapping and navigation techniques from robotics into minimally invasive medical procedures, such as by combining pre-operative CT or MRI as a prior map with sparse intra-operative sensing to help guide bronchoscopes safely to peripheral lung lesions, all while working alongside a physician.

My current research is on optical coherence tomography (OCT), elastography, and custom high-speed micromotor catheters for intravascular coronary artery imaging.  I've gained first-principles understanding of imaging physics and optics, experience building custom catheter hardware, and close familiarity with clinical workflows. I am advised by Professor Néstor Uribe-Patarroyo and Professor Brett E. Bouma in the [Wellman Center for Photomedicine](https://wellman.massgeneral.org/) at Massachusetts General Hospital (MGH) and Harvard Medical School.

I earned my undergraduate degree in general engineering from Harvey Mudd College, where I "dove" into research on autonomous underwater microgliders in the [Lab for Autonomous and Intelligent Robotics (LAIR)](https://www.lair.hmc.edu/). I was advised by robotics professor [Christopher Clark](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/christopher-clark), and completed coursework in robotics state estimation and motion planning. 

I've also interned at [Auris Health](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aurishealth/) on the Advanced Development team working on minimally-invasive, laproscopic surgical robotics. After its [acquisition](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/johnson-johnson-announces-agreement-to-acquire-auris-health-inc) by Johnson & Johnson, I returned in the following years to work with the same team. My industry experience has given me practical experience into the design, development, and translation of medical devices and hardware.

Outside the lab, I competed on BattleBots and was the [first woman](https://news.mit.edu/2023/mit-women-historic-battlebots-runs-0208) to win the Giant Nut Trophy in Season 6 with our robot, [Tantrum](https://battlebots.fandom.com/wiki/Tantrum). 

My long-term goal is to pursue a faculty position in an engineering department with a dual affiliation at an academic research hospital.
